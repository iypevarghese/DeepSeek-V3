WABA "Schedule Bot" Deployment

With 62% of drop-offs occurring in the Appointment IVR, we are deploying an automated WhatsApp Business API (WABA) solution. By integrating a 24/7 chatbot, we provide an immediate alternative for patients who cannot wait in the voice queue.

The "Press 9" Logic

Precision Staffing: The "Bridge Shift"

Our data confirms that fixed shifts fail to manage the 10 AM – 2 PM peak. We are realigning agent schedules to ensure 100% occupancy during these critical four hours, specifically targeting the Monday/Tuesday morning surge.

Morning Peak Alignment

Shift transition now occurs at 2 PM rather than 12 PM to prevent "Agent Gap" during the busiest window.

Bridge Shift Implementation

A dedicated 4-hour micro-shift covering 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM will handle the "High Variance" volume.

📞

Intelligent Queue Callbacks

Patients reach their "patience cliff" at 45 seconds. By offering a Queue Callback at the 30-second mark, we retain the patient relationship even if we cannot answer immediately.

  • ✔ Reduces abandonment by 40% (estimated)
  • ✔ Eliminates "Normal Clearing" drop-offs
  • ✔ Prioritizes callbacks for surgical patients
